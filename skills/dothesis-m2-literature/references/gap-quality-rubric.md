# Gap quality rubric

A research gap candidate ships only if it passes ALL of these.

1. **Specific.** Names the missing construct/context/method — not "more research is
   needed on X". Test: could two different studies both claim to fill it? If yes,
   it's too broad.
2. **Evidenced by ≥2 sources.** The gap emerges from the *combination* of papers
   ("src-001 only studies livestream; src-005 only short video; nobody measures both
   simultaneously") — a single paper saying "future research should…" is that
   author's suggestion, not an established gap.
3. **Typed.** `construct` (missing variable/mechanism) · `context` (population,
   region, platform) · `method` (design limitation, e.g. all cross-sectional) ·
   `conflicting` (papers disagree, unresolved).
4. **Page-cited.** Each supporting source cites the page where the limitation or
   omission is visible. `verified: false` sources cannot carry a gap alone.
5. **RQ-linked.** Connects to at least one M1 research question (`addresses_rq`).
6. **Feasible.** "Addressable as" is a study the user could actually run (sample
   they can access, method within reach). A gap needing a 2-year longitudinal panel
   is honest to list but flag the feasibility cost.

Per round: 3–6 candidates, a spread of types when the literature supports it.
Recommend keeping 2–3 — more dilutes the thesis contribution.
