# Harvest exclusions for the DoThesis blog content engine.
#
# One Python regex per line, matched case-insensitively against the harvest
# keyword and against the competitor URL. A row matching any line is dropped by
# `python -m app.blog.content.cli plan` and never becomes a page.
#
# Never hand-edit the harvest TSVs. Add a rule here instead, with a comment, so
# the reason a row was dropped stays committed and nobody re-adds it by accident.
# Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.

# --- TOEIC and exam answer keys -----------------------------------------------
# phamlocblog ranks a TOEIC answer-key post; `đáp án ets 2016 part 1234` at
# 27,100 is the single biggest row in the harvest and has nothing to do with a
# thesis.
toeic
\bets\b
đáp án ets
đề thi

# --- school algebra -----------------------------------------------------------
# `bậc của đa thức` and friends. Secondary-school maths, wrong audience entirely.
đa thức
đơn thức
thu gọn và tìm bậc
^bậc( là gì)?$
^bậc của

# --- programming --------------------------------------------------------------
# `cú pháp khai báo biến`, `std::`, `regression testing`. The word `biến` collides
# with the statistical sense and drags in programming queries.
cú pháp
khai báo biến
biến cục bộ
std::
regression testing
^binary
^width là gì$

# --- brand and site noise -----------------------------------------------------
# Competitor brand terms, and the junk rows any harvest carries.
^loc$
^locs$
phamlocblog
phạm lộc blog
notebooklm
phần mềm liên thông
^bấm cái
^chắt lọc$
^đưa ra câu trả lời

# --- scam and MLM rows --------------------------------------------------------
# xulysolieu ranks a batch of `<công ty> lừa đảo` pages. Off brand and legally
# fraught.
lừa đảo
đa cấp

# --- software download, crack and version pages -------------------------------
# `tải spss` 1,900 and `spss download` 2,900 are real volume and permanently out
# of scope: instructing on cracked IBM software is not something a product
# students trust with a thesis does. Version pages (`spss 20`, `spss 27`) differ
# by a screenshot, which is the definition of a doorway set.
download
\btải\b
crack
miễn phí
free
for mac
^spss \d+$
^smart ?pls [0-9]$
^stata

# --- other tools we do not support --------------------------------------------
# DoThesis runs SPSS, SmartPLS and AMOS. A page about a tool the product cannot
# help with sends the reader somewhere we cannot follow.
eviews
\bR studio\b
\brstudio\b

# --- done-for-you service queries ---------------------------------------------
# `dịch vụ spss` 1,900 is transactional and DoThesis is not a service. The honest
# comparison belongs inside a how-to page, not on a URL chasing this query.
dịch vụ
thuê chạy
thuê xử lý
giá chạy spss
chạy spss thuê

# --- translation and off-domain definition intent -----------------------------
tiếng anh là gì
significant figure
cung gì

# --- qualitative research -----------------------------------------------------
# DoThesis is quantitative only. A page teaching interviews or coding would
# contradict the product and the writer is forbidden from producing one.
^nghiên cứu định tính
^dữ liệu định tính
phỏng vấn sâu
mã hóa định tính

# --- undiacritised and garbled duplicates -------------------------------------
# `model la gi`, `trung gian la gì`, `d f# là gì`. The proper phrasing of each is
# already in the harvest and clustering keeps the better one.
\bla gi\b
\bla gì\b
là j$
^[a-z](\s|#)+[a-z#]{0,2} là gì$
^(o r|c i|oci|rsd) là gì$

# --- bare words with no answerable intent -------------------------------------
^(biến|nghiên cứu|thu thập|xử lý|xữ lý|số liệu|so lieu|đảo ngược|tuyến tính|định tính|trung gian|reversing|odd|cumulative|categorical|ordinal)$
